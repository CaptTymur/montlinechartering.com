Who We Are

A chartering desk built on the bridge.

Montline Chartering is an independent dry bulk brokerage founded by two Master Mariners who served as commanding officers on cargo vessels.

We didn't learn shipping from a textbook — we lived it on the bridge, in the holds, and through every weather the sea could throw at us. We know how cargo is loaded, how vessels perform in real conditions, and what actually matters when it comes to safe and efficient fixture execution.

We connect cargo with the right tonnage through direct owner relationships, real-time market intelligence and a straightforward approach built on trust and operational expertise.

What We Do

Commercial and post-fixture support.

From cargo enquiry and vessel search to recap and voyage follow-up — handled end to end by people who have stood the bridge watch themselves.

Dry Bulk Freight Brokerage

Matching cargo stems with suitable coasters, river-sea vessels, Handysize and larger dry bulk tonnage across Black Sea, Mediterranean, Baltic, Continent and Caspian.

Voyage & Time Charter Negotiation

Handling the commercial track from freight indication and firm offer to recap — laycan windows, cargo terms, rate negotiations, charter party follow-up for voyage and time charter business.

Post-Fixture Coordination

Keeping owners, charterers, agents and documents aligned after fixture — NOR & laytime monitoring, SoF review, freight invoicing support and demurrage/despatch follow-up.

Specialisation

Market segments and trading area.

Covering the full spectrum of dry bulk tonnage — from river-sea coasters to ocean-going Handysize vessels — with focused regional coverage.

Up to ~10,000 DWT

Coasters & River-Sea

Small-to-medium tonnage for regional trades, river-sea navigation and short-sea shipping across inland waterways and coastal routes.

  • Single-deck general cargo vessels
  • River-sea class tonnage
  • Short-sea and coastal trades
  • Grains, steel, fertilizers, project cargo
20,000+ DWT

Handysize & Above

Ocean-going dry bulk tonnage for intercontinental trades, deep-sea grain flows and larger parcel shipments.

  • Handysize & Supramax bulkers
  • Deep-sea and intercontinental routes
  • Bulk grains, coal, minerals
  • Large parcel and full-cargo lots
