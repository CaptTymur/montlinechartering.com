Company Profile

Montline Chartering

Independent dry bulk brokerage based in Tbilisi, covering coaster, river-sea, and selected Handysize business across the Black Sea, Mediterranean, Baltic, and connected short-sea routes.

What We Actually Do

We work on practical freight problems where cargo, tonnage, timing, and port reality all matter more than generic brokerage wording.

  • Owner-side: market screening, reduced cargo probing, cargo ideas, Baltic / Black Sea / Med cross-coverage.
  • Cargo-side: owner access, parcel-shipment structuring, broker-safe freight checks, routing logic.
  • Execution logic: practical understanding of loading, discharge, restrictions, documentation, laytime exposure, and follow-through after fixture.

Meeting Logic

For a first meeting, we do not need a perfect cargo stem or an open vessel on the table. What matters is whether there is a real operational fit:

  • owners and operators who value an additional market route or cargo access layer;
  • exporters and logistics teams who may benefit from a reserve freight brokerage channel for parcel cargoes or short-sea business;
  • commercial people who prefer practical market talk over decorative networking.

Montline Chartering does not present indicative market interest as firm business and does not disclose unnecessary cargo or vessel fingerprint before a workable basis exists.
